The repo gives you the structured 4-week C++ track. These external resources are the most-cited supplementary material - use them for deeper dives, alternate explanations, and broader question banks.

## Books (Order of Priority)

1. **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** - Martin Kleppmann (the gold standard for the data layer)
2. **System Design Interview Vol 1 + Vol 2** - Alex Xu (concise case-study format)
3. **Head First Design Patterns** - good GoF intro with real examples
4. **Clean Architecture** - Robert C. Martin (SOLID + boundaries)
5. **Designing Distributed Systems** - Brendan Burns (patterns)
6. **Site Reliability Engineering** - Google (free online; reliability mindset)

## Spaced Repetition

Use [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/). The system-design-primer repo includes downloadable decks. Review 10-15 min/day for retention of capacity numbers, latency numbers, and pattern names.

## Reading Roadmap (Parallel to This Repo)

- **Week 1-2 (LLD focus):** Head First Design Patterns + tssovi/grokking-OOD case studies
- **Week 3 (HLD foundations):** Skim Designing Data-Intensive Applications chapters 1-7; one ByteByteGo article daily
- **Week 4 (Case studies):** System Design Interview Vol 1 - read 1 chapter per case study, then re-do that case study from this repo
